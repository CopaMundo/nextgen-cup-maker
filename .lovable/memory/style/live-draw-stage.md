name: Live draw stage
description: Fan podium and organizer controls
type: design
---
- Shared clean 16:9 gold/black studio for fans and organizer, no sound, confetti or fireworks.
- Very small bowl starting around 61% stage height above a centered compact pot table no wider than the central name bar; grounded drifting balls and active pot tag. All active-pot clubs remain visible in an evenly distributed scroll-free grid up to 32 teams, with centered circular logo holders and full centered names wrapping across lines. Drawn clubs stay visible but clearly gray/dimmed.
- Ball opens at the name bar before logo, flag and name appear; no extra placement captions.
- Valid groups only below the team, sweep then hold winner and transfer team to wing.
- Wings never overlap name bar; rounded golden frames and dark transparent slots, no slot numbers, identical fixed circular logo holders.
- Named group layouts use three capped density profiles independently per wing, based on wing rows × internal slot rows: spacious (logo about 2.2cqw, name 1.05cqw, slot 2.6cqw maximum), normal (1.7cqw, .88cqw, 2.1cqw), and compact at heavy load. Golden cards hug the title and slots and center inside their cells; empty stages never exceed the spacious maximum. Compact logo-only layouts and rounds cards keep separate sizing.
- Central reveal shows a centered club logo beside its sharp readable name, with a clearly visible full country-flag watermark to the right of the name (object-contain, including nonstandard/vertical flags); group wings show only club logo and name. The ball travels from the small bowl to the exact name-bar center and splits before revealing the team.
- Organizer has a fullscreen toggle for the complete stage and controls.
- Group draws always show all groups in creation order across both wings. Up to six groups use horizontally centered compact cards (13.5cqw) for one team column and full-wing cards for two internal team columns when needed (limits 6×12, 4×16, 2×32). Each wing centers its actual group count independently; named cards have compact slot-fitted heights capped by available space and remain evenly distributed without artificial stretching, including odd left/right splits. Seven/eight groups use two group columns per wing through eight teams; nine/twelve groups through five teams. Larger formats use compact gold logo tiles without long names; the central reveal retains the full name. No visible scrollbars or audio.
- Organizer primary action sits on bowl; compact bottom actions and settings, invisible to fans.
- Pot tag is a clearly readable gold label, placed lower on the bowl base; bowl balls are larger.
- The central team name is centered on the bar itself, with the logo left and the flag independently right-aligned.
- Rounds use the same studio and bowl: alphabetically ordered active-pot/group roster left, current team's schedule right, known matches immediately visible and remaining opponents revealed per click or automatically. Draw pot 1 before pot 2 and reveal each team's opponents before drawing the next team.
- Rounds default to the format's configured count and remain editable in the live-draw settings. Label the left roster with the active group name. The right card shows the revealed team's identity and opponent rows with pot labels, logos, flags and home/away icons; identities stay hidden until the reveal finishes. Previously known opponents stay in fixed top positions in every draw method; open positions follow in pot order, never round order.
- Left uses the available stage height without an outer frame or background; only the title has a subtle gold frame, with separate team cards in the continuous autoscroll below. Every active-pot/group team appears alphabetically from the beginning, initially dimmed; only after all that team's fixtures finish revealing does its block become gold and all logo-coin badges appear together. Large logo left, readable top-aligned name and separate badge rows: up to 10 rounds one row, 11–20 rounds two rows of at most 10 badges. The bowl's team overview is alphabetical too. Right uses fixed-height opponent cards in a vertically centered block, one centered column through 10 fixtures and two columns of at most 10 for 11–20, with readable logos/names, flags immediately after names and venue icons at the far right.
- Live draw remains accessible above 20 configured rounds: keep groups-only draw available, dim/disable full and rounds-only draws with an explanation that the rounds live show supports at most 20 rounds and the tournament rounds are generated automatically.
- After drawing a team, the organizer explicitly starts Loot speelrondes; choose automatic sequential reveals or manual match-by-match control. Each revealed row appears fully populated with logo, name, flag and home/away icon, never an animated empty intermediate row.
