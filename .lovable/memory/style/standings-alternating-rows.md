---
name: Uniform standings rows
description: Public group standings use one uniform row background without zebra striping; qualification markers remain independent.
type: design
---
Every team row in public group standings uses the same background color. Do not alternate row backgrounds by odd/even position.

Implementation:
- This applies to full group standings, expanded homepage standings, and compact homepage standings.
- Favorite-team emphasis may use text styling, but must not change the row background.
- Qualification-zone indicators are unrelated and remain unchanged.

This applies to every broadcast theme.
